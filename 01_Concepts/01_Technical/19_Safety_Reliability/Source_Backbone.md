## Important scope note
Standards are domain-specific. For a real project, identify the applicable standard, edition, contractual/regulatory scope, and tailoring before treating a concept as a compliance requirement. The current ISO site also lists draft third-edition ISO 26262 documents under development, while published 2018 editions remain listed for the existing series.
