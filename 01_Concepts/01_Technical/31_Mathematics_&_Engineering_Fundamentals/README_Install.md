# Mathematics & Engineering Fundamentals Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook covering the mathematics most useful for a long-term embedded/systems engineering career.

The emphasis is engineering mathematics rather than pure academic mathematics: dimensional reasoning, linear algebra, calculus, probability/statistics, numerical methods, optimization, discrete mathematics, transforms, measurement uncertainty, physical models, control, reliability, queueing, and Staff-level quantitative reasoning.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface.
