# Source Backbone

The package is structured and paraphrased; it is not a reproduction of any standard.

- MISRA: https://www.misra.org.uk/

## Source scope notes
ISO currently lists the 10-part ISO 26262:2018 package, including management, concept, system, hardware, software, supporting processes, analyses, and guidelines. IEC currently lists IEC 62304:2006+AMD1:2015 as the consolidated publication. ISO lists ISO 14971:2019 as the third edition for medical-device risk management. AIAG describes the AIAG & VDA FMEA Handbook as a harmonized guide for Design FMEA, Process FMEA, and supplemental FMEA.
