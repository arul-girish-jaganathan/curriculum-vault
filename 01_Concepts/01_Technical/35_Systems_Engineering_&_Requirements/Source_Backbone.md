# Source Backbone

This package is a structured, paraphrased study framework. It is not a verbatim reproduction of any standard.

## Primary references checked

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 — Systems and software engineering — Requirements engineering: https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html
- INCOSE Systems Engineering Handbook: https://www.incose.org/resources-publications/technical-publications/se-handbook/

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 Architecture Description: https://www.iso.org/standard/50508.html

## Current-source note
ISO lists ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 as the published second edition and describes it as a framework of life-cycle processes spanning conception, development, production, utilization, support, retirement, acquisition and supply. ISO lists ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 as the requirements-engineering edition and also shows a newer draft international standard under development. INCOSE identifies its fifth-edition Systems Engineering Handbook as aligned to ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023.
