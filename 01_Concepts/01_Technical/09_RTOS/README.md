# RTOS Textbook — Obsidian 90 Chapters / Complete Topic Map

90 chapter folders × 12 canonical topic notes = 1,080 topic notes.

The scope covers:
- RTOS fundamentals and real-time theory
- tasks, TCBs, stacks and context switches
- scheduling and schedulability
- ISR/SVC/PendSV/SysTick and Cortex-M ports
- synchronization, IPC and priority protocols
- timing, timers and tickless operation
- memory, pools and fault recovery
- SMP/AMP and multicore
- low power, watchdogs, diagnostics and tracing
- drivers, DMA and networking
- Linux/PREEMPT_RT, Zephyr and FreeRTOS
- safety, testing and Staff-level architecture
