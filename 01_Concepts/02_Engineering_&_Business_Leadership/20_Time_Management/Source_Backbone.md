# Source Backbone

This package is a structured, paraphrased study framework rather than a reproduction of any single book or productivity methodology.

## Reference themes
- Time management and productivity principles
- Goal setting and prioritization
- Deep work and attention management
- Delegation and managerial leverage
- Habit formation and behavior design
- Executive calendar and strategic time allocation
- Personal operating systems and review cadences

The emphasis is on durable principles that can be adapted rather than strict adherence to one productivity system.
