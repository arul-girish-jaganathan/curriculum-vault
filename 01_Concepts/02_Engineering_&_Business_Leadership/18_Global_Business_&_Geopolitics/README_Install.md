# Global Business & Geopolitics Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for long-term business, geopolitical, macroeconomic, technology-policy, supply-chain, capital-allocation, and CEO-level strategic thinking.

This domain intentionally complements the 30-domain technical engineering curriculum rather than extending it. The goal is to build business judgment for leading a global technology company.
