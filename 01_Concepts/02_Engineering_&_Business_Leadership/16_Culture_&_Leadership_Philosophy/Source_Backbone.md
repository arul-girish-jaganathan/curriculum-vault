# Source Backbone

This package is a structured, paraphrased study framework. It is not a reproduction of any single book or source.

- The Toyota Way / continuous improvement concepts: https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/
- Google re:Work resources: https://rework.withgoogle.com/
