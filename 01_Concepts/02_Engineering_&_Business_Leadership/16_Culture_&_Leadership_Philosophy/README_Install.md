# 16 Culture & Leadership Philosophy — Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for long-term leadership and culture development from Staff/Principal Engineer through Engineering Executive and CEO.

This is intentionally focused on the leadership operating system: principles, behaviors, incentives, people systems, communication, decision making, organizational design, culture under pressure, and CEO-level judgment.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep personal leadership reflections, real company situations, decisions, lessons, and case studies in separate notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.
