# Business Strategy Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for business strategy and CEO-level strategic thinking.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep company-specific strategy documents, board materials, market research, business cases, investment theses, and operating reviews in separate notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.

The scope spans strategy foundations, industry/market analysis, business models, competitive advantage, product/GTM, operations, technology strategy, finance, capital allocation, risk, M&A, organization, culture, execution, global strategy, technology disruption, and CEO-level judgment.
