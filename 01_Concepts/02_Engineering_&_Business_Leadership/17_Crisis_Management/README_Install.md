# Crisis Management Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for executive crisis management, business continuity, incident response, communications, product and technology crises, cyber events, financial crises, regulatory/legal crises, supply-chain disruption, recovery, resilience, and CEO-level leadership.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep company-specific crisis plans, incident reports, war-room records, customer communications, RCA/CAPA, and exercises in separate operational notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.

This is a leadership and engineering reference framework, not a substitute for legal counsel, regulatory direction, emergency services, or company-specific crisis procedures.
