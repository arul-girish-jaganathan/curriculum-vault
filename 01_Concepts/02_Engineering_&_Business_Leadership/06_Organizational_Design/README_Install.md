# Organizational Design Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for organizational design, operating models, team structure, leadership architecture, talent systems, governance, transformation, and CEO-level organizational thinking.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep company-specific org charts, reorganization proposals, operating-model decisions, people cases, and executive notes separate and link them back to these canonical concepts.

The goal is durable organizational understanding rather than memorizing one company's structure.
