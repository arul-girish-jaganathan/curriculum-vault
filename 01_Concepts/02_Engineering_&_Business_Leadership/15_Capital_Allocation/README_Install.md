# Capital Allocation Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for executive-level Capital Allocation.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep company-specific investment cases, board materials, budgets, acquisition models, and capital reviews in project/business notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.

The focus is CEO-level judgment: turning strategy into capital deployment, comparing competing uses of cash, protecting downside, measuring realized returns, and compounding enterprise value over the long term.
