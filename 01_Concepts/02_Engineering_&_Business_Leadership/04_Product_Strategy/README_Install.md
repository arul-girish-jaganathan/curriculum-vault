# 04 Product Strategy Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for Product Strategy, designed for a long-term engineering-to-executive/CEO curriculum.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep product-specific strategy memos, customer research, pricing experiments, portfolio decisions, launch reviews, and business cases in separate notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.

This domain complements the technical curriculum by covering customer value, market strategy, product economics, GTM alignment, portfolio decisions, execution, and CEO-level product judgment.
