# Decision Making & Judgment Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for long-term executive decision-making and judgment.

This domain is intentionally positioned after the technical and engineering curriculum. It focuses on the transition from solving technical problems to making high-leverage decisions across technology, product, people, capital, strategy, risk, governance, crises, and long-term company building.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface.
