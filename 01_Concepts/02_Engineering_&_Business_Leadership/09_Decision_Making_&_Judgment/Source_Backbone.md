# Source Backbone

This package is a structured, paraphrased study framework. It is not a verbatim reproduction of any single book or source.

## Primary references
- Harvard Business Review: https://hbr.org/
- Stanford Graduate School of Business: https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/
- MIT Sloan Management Review: https://sloanreview.mit.edu/
- Farnam Street / mental models: https://fs.blog/
- Center for Applied Rationality resources: https://www.rationality.org/
- Kahneman, Slovic & Tversky research on judgment and decision making
- Annie Duke resources on decision quality and probabilistic thinking
- Philip Tetlock research on forecasting and judgment
- Richard Rumelt resources on strategy and diagnosis
- Clayton Christensen resources on innovation and disruption
- Good Judgment Project resources: https://goodjudgment.com/
