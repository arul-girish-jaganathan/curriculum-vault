## Curriculum framing
The curriculum covers BATNA, interests vs positions, anchoring, ZOPA, concessions, leverage, stakeholder mapping, principled negotiation, calibrated questioning, coalition building, conflict diagnosis/resolution, executive communication, decision making under uncertainty, organizational power, commercial negotiation, and ethical influence. The goal is durable executive judgment rather than tactical manipulation.
