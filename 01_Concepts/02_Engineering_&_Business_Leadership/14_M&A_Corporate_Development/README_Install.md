# 14_M&A_Corporate_Development Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for Corporate Development and M&A, designed for long-term executive/CEO-level business understanding.

The focus is not investment-banking memorization. It is the ability to reason about strategy, targets, diligence, valuation, deal structure, negotiation, regulatory risk, integration, synergy realization, capital allocation, boards, and enterprise value creation.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface.
