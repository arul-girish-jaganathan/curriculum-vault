# 13_Competitive Intelligence Obsidian Knowledge Base

A 90-chapter canonical textbook for Competitive Intelligence, market intelligence, strategy, competitor analysis, technology intelligence, financial intelligence, ecosystem intelligence, geopolitical awareness, executive decision support, and Staff/CEO-level strategic thinking.

Use `00_Complete_Topic_Map.md` as the primary navigation surface. Keep company-specific competitor profiles, market models, confidential strategy work, board materials, account intelligence, and intelligence reports in project/business notes and link them back to these canonical concepts.

This is a long-term knowledge framework, not a substitute for legal advice, licensed data subscriptions, regulated market research processes, or confidential corporate information.
