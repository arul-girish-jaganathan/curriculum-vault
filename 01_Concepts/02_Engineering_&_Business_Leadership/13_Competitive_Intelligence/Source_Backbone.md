# Source Backbone

This package is a structured, paraphrased study framework. It is not a reproduction of any source.

## Public-source reference families
- SEC EDGAR: https://www.sec.gov/edgar
- Investor relations filings and presentations
- Patent databases / USPTO: https://www.uspto.gov/patents
- EPO Espacenet: https://worldwide.espacenet.com/
- WIPO Patentscope: https://patentscope.wipo.int/
- U.S. International Trade Commission: https://www.usitc.gov/
- World Trade Organization: https://www.wto.org/
- OECD data and industry resources: https://www.oecd.org/
- World Bank data: https://data.worldbank.org/
- U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis: https://www.bea.gov/
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: https://www.bls.gov/
- Company annual reports / regulatory filings
- Investor presentations and earnings-call materials
- Public product documentation and release notes
- Public job postings
- Public standards and regulatory publications

## Methodology references
- Strategic management and competitive strategy literature
- Market research methodology
- Scenario planning and strategic foresight literature
- Intelligence-analysis methodology
- FMEA and risk-analysis concepts where competitive risk overlaps operational risk

## Governance note
Competitive intelligence should use lawful, ethical, and appropriately sourced information. Preserve source provenance and distinguish evidence from analyst inference.
