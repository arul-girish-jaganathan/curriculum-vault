## 7. Versioning Principle

The knowledge base should evolve rather than restart:

```text
V1
 ↓
experience
 ↓
corrections
 ↓
new evidence
 ↓
V2
 ↓
new projects
 ↓
new technologies
 ↓
V3
```

Preserve meaningful history.
